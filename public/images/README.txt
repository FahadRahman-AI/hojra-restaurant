HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTOS
======================

To add your images, simply place your photo files in this folder
(public/images/) using the exact filenames below. The on-screen
placeholders will automatically be replaced the next time the page
is refreshed — no code changes are required.

Required filenames
------------------
  hero.jpg        Hero background — restaurant interior or signature dish
                  (wide / landscape works best)

  about.jpg       Story section photo (portrait / tall works best)

  dish-1.jpg      Signature dish highlight (suggested: Chinaki teapot stew)

  gallery-1.jpg   Gallery photo 1  (e.g. Qabli Pilau)
  gallery-2.jpg   Gallery photo 2  (e.g. Chapli Kebab)
  gallery-3.jpg   Gallery photo 3  (e.g. the dining room)

Tips
----
- Use .jpg files with these exact names (all lowercase).
- Large, high-quality photos look best (1600px wide or more).
- After copying the files in, refresh the website to see them.
